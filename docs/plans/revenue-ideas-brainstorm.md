# Revenue Ideas — Brutal Brainstorm

> **Status: WORK IN PROGRESS.** Idea generation, not commitments. Planning doc — not site content, not rendered. Lives in the repo; if public, this file is visible.

The brief: novel, realistic, honest ways to generate/mine/extract money — passive or active — as a product, service, or tool. In-industry or completely separate. Untapped markets/communities. Open to "selling your soul" and to automated design tooling, but think outside the box.

---

## The filters (how every idea below is judged)

Four brutal truths govern this, and they kill most "great ideas" before effort does:

1. **Distribution is the constraint, not the idea.** You can build almost anything. You cannot currently *sell* anything cold — no audience, no funnel, and a stated unwillingness to do outbound. So the only ideas that survive are ones where distribution comes from something *other than* your willingness to market: a marketplace's own traffic, a warm network, or a macro wave that delivers a captive audience.
2. **"Passive" is mostly a myth.** Income that needs no time also needs no marketing, and gets no sales. The closest-to-passive things (digital downloads) still trend to zero without a demand source. Judge "passive" as "low ongoing time *given* a distribution source that's also low-time."
3. **Your real, underused distribution assets are exactly three:** (a) your writing, (b) the emerging MACH/speaking network, (c) the macro *fear* among designers that makes them a captive audience for your specific expertise. Winning ideas monetize one of these. Losing ideas assume you'll become a salesperson. You won't.
4. **Your edges:** you build fast with AI, you have taste/judgment, you've built real agent infrastructure (Genie), you write well, and you have 13 years of enterprise/agency scar tissue. Your constraints: off-hours capacity, safety preference, the Orium conflict, and zero audience-building appetite so far.

Shorthand per idea: **Fit** (to you) · **Distribution** (built-in? or from-zero) · **Income** (passive/active) · **Ceiling** · **Verdict**.

**4. "Become an AI-native design engineer" course / cohort.** For the millions of designers terrified the bifurcation leaves them behind. Your thesis (*Taste is Triage*), your proof (Genie, token pipeline), your writing — packaged into a paid path from "Figma-only designer" to "resilient tier."
Fit 5 · Distribution: your writing + the fear itself · Income: passive (recorded) scaling to active (cohort) · Ceiling: **high** (hot-niche course creators make 6–7 figures) · **Verdict: the single highest-EV non-job idea for you.** It monetizes all three of your real distribution assets at once, and the audience is large, scared, and paying. Caveat: needs you to publish consistently and run at least one launch — the flywheel you've already started (MACH, CSS-Tricks) is the prerequisite, not an afterthought.

**5. Paid community / "guild" for designers reskilling into design-eng/AX.** Recurring membership, lower churn than a one-off course, you as the authority-in-residence.
Fit 4 · Distribution: content + course · Income: recurring · Ceiling: modest-high · **Verdict: the natural *sequel* to #4, not the opener. Communities die without a critical mass you don't have yet. Earn it with the course first.**

## Synthesis — the portfolio, not the pick

Don't choose one. Run a portfolio with the right shape: **one reliable active engine funding a few cheap, distribution-advantaged bets, plus one bigger swing.**

- **Engine (fund everything, de-risk the base):** premium AI-design-eng contracting (#15) — *or* leverage-in-place at Orium, whichever pays more. This is the boring money that buys you the runway to try the rest.
- **Best-EV real bet:** the **AI-native design engineer education play (#4)** — it monetizes your writing, your network, and the macro fear simultaneously, and it's the one with a genuinely high ceiling that fits how you operate.
- **Cheapest compounding shots (do in parallel, low risk):** agent config packs (#1) and a Figma plugin (#8) — low-maintenance, marketplace/content distribution, and they feed #4.
- **The big swing (only with a wedge):** Genie-for-a-vertical (#6) or design-partner-to-AI-startups (#7) — highest ceiling, but pull the trigger only where distribution is already half-solved.
- **The sleeper:** the MACH/composable toolkit (#11) — because a warm network is worth more to you than a bigger cold market.

## The one honest meta-truth

Every idea's fate is decided by distribution, and you have exactly three distribution assets: your writing, your emerging network, and other designers' fear. The ideas that win all monetize one of those. The ideas that lose all quietly assume you'll wake up one day willing to cold-sell. Build where the distribution already exists — and everything else is a spreadsheet.
